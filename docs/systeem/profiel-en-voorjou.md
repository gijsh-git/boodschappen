# Aankoopprofiel, favorieten en Voor jou

> Deel van de systeembeschrijving; de index staat in `CLAUDE.md`. Een verwijzing als (see "Offers") gaat naar een kopje in dit bestand of in een ander bestand in `docs/systeem/`; de index zegt welk. Werk dit bestand bij in dezelfde commit als de wijziging die het gedrag verandert.

## Profile screen

The `profiel` view is opened from the tab bar. The header shows your initial, name and e-mail address with a pencil button to edit the display name (first entered once in the `naam` view). Below it the purchase profile (see "Purchase profile"), "Mijn favorieten" (see "Favourites") and a menu "Account" with "Koppelingen beheren" (admin only, see "Product types") and "Uitloggen".

## Purchase profile

The section "Aankoopprofiel" in the `profiel` view shows what the household buys. The database does all the arithmetic: `loadAankoopprofiel()` calls the `security definer` RPC `purchase_profile(p_period, p_list)` (`'4w'`, `'3m'`, `'12m'` or `'alles'`, rolling from today; one list or null for all) and `renderAankoopprofiel()` only displays the JSON it returns. It counts purchases from lists you are a member of with `counts_for_profile`, archived ones included, and it is a household profile: everything bought on the list, also before you joined. A purchase counts under its product type through `purchase_types()` (the article on the receipt first, then the name; see "Product types"); a purchase without a type counts under its own name. `product_types.counts_in_profile` (default true) is the flag "telt niet mee in profiel" for things like a carrier bag, set with the switch in the opened type in the screen "Koppelingen" (`update_product_type()`): flagged types are left out of the purchase count, the product count and the top 10, but stay in spent and saved. `price` is the whole line before discount, so spent is `price - discount` and saved is `discount`, over purchases with a price only. The top 10 is ordered by purchase days in the period; the interval per product is the median gap between purchase days over all data (the list filter applies, the period does not), shown from 3 purchase days. Stores come from the receipt (`receipts.store`, grouped case-insensitively; none is "onbekend"). The month bars always cover the last 12 months. The key figures are always visible; the top 10, stores, months and the explanation "Hoe wordt dit berekend?" are `<details class="ap-blok">` blocks, collapsed by default. All days and months are Europe/Amsterdam. The bars are plain CSS (`staafRij()`), no chart library. `profielVraag` makes a late answer harmless. `docs/aankoopprofiel-controle.sql` holds a check query per figure for the SQL Editor.

## Favourites

A favourite is something the user wants to hear about when it is on offer: a product type, a brand plus a type, or only a brand. The table stores what the user picked or typed, a term, a brand, or both (`favorites`: `user_id`, `term`, `brand`, `category`, plus the generated columns `normalized_term` and `normalized_brand`). They are per user, not per list: RLS only gives you your own rows, and the client writes the table directly (no RPC). The database refuses a row without term and brand and, through the unique index `favorites_uniek`, the same term and brand twice. `normalize_search(text)` (lower case, no accents through the `unaccent` extension, spaces collapsed) is the one normalisation for favourites; `Logica.zoekvorm()` mirrors it in the client. The section "Mijn favorieten" in the `profiel` view is the only place to add, edit (pencil, fills the form) and delete them. State and rules are in `logica.js` (`Logica.bewaarFavoriet()`, `verwijderFavoriet()`, `favorietSuggesties()`); `app.js` only renders (`renderFavorieten()`, `favorietRij()`) and handles the form. While typing, `suggest_favorite_terms(p_query)` (`security definer`, because the type list is not readable for the app roles) suggests product types by name, with the main group as `category` and the number of articles of the type as `articles`; `suggest_favorite_brands(p_query)` (`security invoker`) suggests brands from `articles.brand` ("AH" and "AH Excellent" are separate brands). `category` is only stored when the term was picked from the suggestions and is dropped when the term is changed afterwards (`favSuggestie`); there is no input for it, and it only counts for a term that stands for no type. What a term stands for is decided when matching, not stored: `offers_for_me()` resolves it with `name_type()`, so a correction by the admin or a merged type works through by itself ("tomatensoep" follows soep). A term that stands for a type hits every article of that type in an offer, also when the word is not in the title, and with a brand only that brand's articles within the type; a brand alone hits everything of the brand; a term that stands for nothing falls back to its words in the title plus the main category. The full definition is in `ROADMAP.md`, step 4.

## Voor jou

Two parts. On top "Nu in de bonus": `loadAanbod()` calls `Logica.laadAanbiedingen()`, which keeps the JSON of `offers_for_me()` (see "Matching offers"); `aanbodRij()` shows per offer the discount text, title, store, last valid day and the reason (your favourite, or the regular products in it), with a plus that puts the offer itself on the open list under its title (`Logica.aanbiedingNaam()`, the title without the trailing asterisk), never the name of the regular product: `zetOpLijst(naam, aanbiedingId)` calls `add_offer_item` (see "Matching offers"). An offer that is already on the list (same name, or an item with its `offer_id`) shows "staat op de lijst" instead of the plus. Below it the household's regular products: `Logica.laadVoorJou()` calls `purchase_profile('3m', null)` and keeps its top 10. `Logica.voorJouOver()` leaves out what is already on the open list (same name, case-insensitive, or an item with the `offer_id` of an offer that contains the product) or was dismissed with "Niet nu" (`Logica.slaOver()`, in memory only) and sorts the product that is furthest past its usual interval (`om_de`) first; `renderVoorJou()` puts that one in the large card; `zetOpLijst()` inserts it into `items` of the open list under the product name, without article or product id.
