# Lijsten, leden en inloggen

> Deel van de systeembeschrijving; de index staat in `CLAUDE.md`. Een verwijzing als (see "Offers") gaat naar een kopje in dit bestand of in een ander bestand in `docs/systeem/`; de index zegt welk. Werk dit bestand bij in dezelfde commit als de wijziging die het gedrag verandert.

## Multiple lists

The `setup` view is the overview "Mijn lijsten": it lists your lists (tap to switch) and is reachable from the list view through the list menu: tapping the list name in the header (`lijsten-knop`) opens a small menu (`lijst-menu`, `toonLijstMenu()`) with "Mijn lijsten", "Deelnemers" (the panel that the avatars also open) and, for who may invite, "Iemand uitnodigen". A choice, a tap next to it, Escape or another view closes the menu. Its plus button opens the `nieuw` view, which holds the forms to create a list or join by pasting an invitation link. Each row has a switch "Meetellen voor aankoopprofiel", stored in `lists.counts_for_profile` (default true) through the `set_list_profile` RPC. The setting belongs to the list, so it is shared by all members; it only decides whether the list counts in the purchase profile (`purchase_profile` reads it); purchases are always stored (see "Purchases").

## Archiving and deleting

Archiving is the normal way to get rid of a list, because deleting takes its purchases and receipts along (`on delete cascade`), and those are the basis of the purchase profile. The creator sees "Lijst archiveren" per row and, right of it, a bin button that calls `delete_list` after a confirm that warns about this (`verwijderLijst()`). Archiving calls `archive_list(p_list, p_archived)` and sets `lists.archived_at`. Archived lists leave the overview and show up in the collapsible block "Gearchiveerd" below it (`archiefRij()`), where the creator can put them back with "Terugzetten". Nothing can be added to an archived list: the insert policy on `items`, `join_list` and `save_receipt` all refuse it. Purchases and receipts stay and keep counting for the profile. A member who has the list open when it is archived is sent back to the overview by `verlaatLijst()`, triggered by the realtime UPDATE on `lists` or, after standby, by `controleerArchief()` in `loadItems()`.

## Members

The header of the list view shows a circle with the initial of each member (`leden-knop`), which toggles `leden-paneel` above the add form (`toonLedenPaneel()`) with who is in the list. `renderLeden()` renders both from the module-level `leden` array (names come from `namen`). `loadLeden()` runs alongside `loadItems()`, and the realtime channel refetches on a `list_members` INSERT and drops the row on DELETE. Only the creator sees "Verwijderen" per member (the `remove_member` RPC) and "Beheerder maken" / "Geen beheerder meer" (`set_member_manager`, stored in `list_members.is_manager`); a realtime UPDATE on `list_members` refetches the members. Everyone except the creator sees "Lijst verlaten" per row in the overview (`lijstVerlaten()`, RPC `leave_list`, which also drops the invitations that person had open); the creator cannot leave and archives or deletes the list instead. The purchases stay on the list, but stop counting for the leaver's purchase profile.

## Invitations

Only the creator and the managers (`can_invite()`, mirrored in the client by `magUitnodigen()`) see "Iemand uitnodigen" in the list menu (`uitnodig-knop`). It calls `create_invite(p_list)`, which stores a row in `list_invites` (token, 7 days valid, single use) and returns the token; the client shows the link `…?uitnodiging=<token>` in the panel with a "Delen" button (`navigator.share`, or copy to the clipboard). `list_invites` has no policies or grants: it is only touched by the RPCs. Opening the link stores the token in `localStorage` under `bonusbuddy-uitnodiging` and removes it from the address bar; `route()` then calls `verwerkUitnodiging()`, which runs `join_list(p_code)` once you are logged in and opens that list. `join_list` deletes the invitation when someone joins with it (not when you already were a member). Removing a member or taking away the manager role deletes the invitations that person still had open. The invited person needs an account (signups are disabled). When your own membership disappears (removed by the creator, or the list is gone or archived), `verlaatLijst()` closes the list and returns to the overview. The DELETE handler checks `list_id` itself rather than trusting the channel filter.

## Auth is invite-only with passwords

Signups are disabled in the Supabase dashboard; the owner adds people there with "Invite user". Login is `signInWithPassword`. "Wachtwoord vergeten?" goes `login` → `forgot` (request the mail) → `sent` (confirmation, with resend). Invite and reset mails land back on the app with `type=invite` / `type=recovery` in the URL hash, which `app.js` reads before it calls `Data.init()` to create the Supabase client (the client clears the hash). That sets `mustSetPassword`, which makes `route()` show the `password` view instead of the list until `updateUser({ password })` succeeds. Supabase's English auth errors are mapped to Dutch in `nl()`.
